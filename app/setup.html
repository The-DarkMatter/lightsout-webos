With your phone

Scan the QR code. The page sets up a one-time bookmark. After that, signing in is: log in on formula1.com on your phone, then tap the bookmark. Stay on this screen while you do it. If your phone asks for a pairing code, it's the one under the QR code.

With the TV browser

Log in on formula1.com in the TV's web browser, then come back to this app. It picks the sign-in up on its own.

On this PC

  1. Log in on formula1.com in this browser.
  2. On that tab, press F12 and open Application → Cookies, then copy the value of login-session.
  3. Paste it below. The whole cookie value works, or just the token inside it.
